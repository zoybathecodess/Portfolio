About

Building things that learn.

I'm an AI & ML engineering student who likes problems where the right answer isn't hand-coded — it's learned. My work sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, applied machine learning, and software engineering: teaching a rover to conserve battery through DQN and PPO, building an XGBoost model that scores seller trustworthiness beyond star ratings, and forecasting atmospheric CO2 for sustainability research.

Outside the notebook, I compete. I've gone through 24-hour hackathons against 200+ participants — building a trust-scoring ML model in one, a battery-aware rover navigation system in another — and pitched product ideas at ideathons, which keeps me equally comfortable in a research mindset and a build-fast one.

I'm looking for teams where I can keep doing both: rigorous ML research and shipping software that people actually use.
